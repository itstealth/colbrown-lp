A Century of Shaping
India's Finest Men

We believe a truly educated young man is more than his marks. He is honest, resilient, curious and compassionate. Every policy, programme and tradition at CBS is designed with that end in mind.

Common Questions

Everything Parents Ask
Before Saying Yes

What is the fee structure for boarding? +
The annual boarding and tuition fee is structured to cover all essential residential services — accommodation, meals, laundry, medical support and co-curricular activities. To receive the current detailed fee schedule, please submit your enquiry above or call +91 63951 14363. Our Admissions team will share the fee structure privately with no obligation.
How does the school ensure my son's safety on campus? +
CBS operates a 24×7 supervised residential campus. Each house has a dedicated Housemaster and a trained Matron who live on-campus. A resident Medical Officer runs the school infirmary. The campus has security personnel and CCTV coverage. Parents are notified immediately in any situation, and regular parent-teacher communication is a structural part of boarding life.
Which board does Col Brown Cambridge School follow? +
CBS is affiliated to the ICSE board. Students appear for ICSE (Class X) and ISC (Class XII) examinations — widely recognised as one of India's most rigorous and respected school-level boards, accepted by universities globally.
What does a typical day in boarding look like? +
A CBS day begins with morning PT and assembly, moves through academic classes, activity periods, sports, supervised prep (study hall) in the evening, and ends with house time before lights-out. The structured routine is intentional — routine creates discipline, and discipline creates freedom. Boys know exactly what to expect, and thrive within that structure.
Does the school offer coaching for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, or NDA? +
Yes — CBS is one of the few boarding schools in Dehradun to offer fully integrated, on-campus coaching for JEE Main & Advanced, NEET-UG, and NDA entrance examinations. These sessions are conducted by specialist faculty without disrupting the regular academic timetable or boarding routine.
Can we visit the campus before applying? +
Absolutely. We strongly encourage campus visits — nothing conveys the CBS experience like walking through our grounds, meeting the housemasters, and speaking with our Admissions team in person. You can also request a virtual tour if you're travelling from outside Dehradun. Submit your enquiry above and we'll schedule your visit at a time that suits you.
